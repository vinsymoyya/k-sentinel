# Small residual CNN: research control

**Version:** 0.1.0. **Author:** Vinsy Moyya. **Purpose:** an inexpensive trained baseline for a synthetic reconstruction-audit benchmark. **Excluded uses:** diagnosis, treatment, scanner control, patient triage and clinical quality assurance.

Four 3x3 convolutions with channels 2-16-16-16-2 and ReLU between hidden layers process real and imaginary image channels. A residual connection adds the input. A Fourier projection then replaces initially acquired coefficients with the measured data. This is not a state-of-the-art MRI reconstruction network. Three separately seeded models are trained eight epochs on 512 phantoms each, with batch size 32, Adam at 0.001, and mean squared complex-channel loss. The fixed eight-epoch model is used; there is no best-test or validation checkpoint selection.

Input is a complex zero-filled reconstruction from 16/64 Cartesian columns, including six central columns. Audit values and audit mask identities never enter CNN training or prediction. Training masks and noise are fixed per training phantom. No augmentation across epochs, pretrained model, or external data is used. Checkpoints contain weights, seed, training history and configuration. Checkpoint hashes are recorded in the release manifest. Load trusted checkpoints using `torch.load(path, weights_only=True, map_location='cpu')`.

The calibration population is the clean synthetic generator, separate from training and test identities. Clean here means **no deliberately injected challenge**, not error-free anatomy: the CNN has native reconstruction error. The audit threshold therefore controls departures from the baseline score distribution, rather than guaranteeing small anatomical error. A large native error can mask a smaller added error. Calibration guarantees are marginal under exchangeability and do not apply automatically to changed noise or anatomy.

The oracle reconstruction is a mathematical control that starts from exact ground truth before inserting observed measurement noise. It is unavailable in real use. Zero filling is a nonlearned baseline. Compare the CNN's practical results separately from oracle results. Summary statistics, all failure categories and per-seed rates are in `results/main/summary.json` and the paper. No subgroup fairness, patient outcome, real-device latency or natural pathology detection evaluation has been performed.

## Separate v0.3.0 real-MRI pilot

The original phantom weights above are unchanged. A separate M4Raw fine-tuned checkpoint and native centered multicoil adapter are documented in [the real-pilot card](research/real-acquisition/MODEL_CARD.md). It trains only on twenty separate healthy volunteers and does not outperform the frozen CNN in the primary reference-error comparison. All participant roles are disjoint. These results are exploratory; clinical validation and focal-feature labels are absent. M4Raw attribution/CC BY 4.0 data licensing remain separate from MIT code/weights and the older CC0 synthetic data.
